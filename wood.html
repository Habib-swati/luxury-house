Types of Wood & Timber

A short guide to the wood we use in our workshop.

Teak (Sagwan)

Very strong and water resistant. Best for main doors and outdoor furniture.

Rs 4,500 / cubic ft

Walnut (Akhrot)

Dark brown colour with beautiful grain. Used for carved doors and luxury furniture.

Rs 5,200 / cubic ft

Oak

Hard and heavy wood. Good for window frames, floors and strong tables.

Rs 3,800 / cubic ft

Sheesham (Rosewood)

Popular local wood. Does not bend easily and takes polish very well.

Rs 3,200 / cubic ft

Pine (Deodar)

Light and cheap wood. Good for ceilings, simple doors and packing work.

Rs 9,000 to 12,000 / cubic ft

Mango Wood

Soft and easy to cut. Used for small furniture and low budget projects.

Rs 1,200 / cubic ft
